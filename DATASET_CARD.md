# Dataset Card

No verified Indian Sign Language dataset is included in this repository. Raw and processed data
paths are ignored because webcam samples may contain biometric/personal data.

- Supported labels: not yet established
- Split: not yet performed on genuine ISL data
- Accuracy, precision, recall, macro F1, weighted F1: **NOT YET MEASURED**

Before training, select or collect appropriately licensed ISL data, verify labels and provenance,
and separate signer/session groups across splits. ASL data must not be presented as ISL. Dynamic
signs require a temporal model and must not be claimed as solved by the static-frame classifier.
